## 2. What Ember does

Ember helps you keep track of how long it has been since you last exchanged with people you care
about. It works entirely on your device. It has no account system and no server.

Optionally, and only with your explicit permission, Ember can detect contact automatically by
reading notifications from WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram. This is described in full in
the Privacy Policy and in the in-app disclosure screen. The App is fully usable without granting
