5.3 Trial

A 14-day trial is available. During the trial, all premium features are available.

When the trial ends, nothing is deleted. If you are tracking more than five friends, you choose which five remain active; the others are paused. No payment method is registered to start the trial, and the trial never converts into a purchase on its own.

The trial can be used once. The App keeps two timestamps recording that it has run, and these survive the in-app "Erase all my data" action for that reason. They contain nothing personal.
